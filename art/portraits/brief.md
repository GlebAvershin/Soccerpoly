## Запрос художника

Paint a production character texture sheet, a SINGLE atlas in landscape 3:2 exactly three equal columns and two equal rows. Six large front elevation HEADS for a premium football videogame. First row left to right: Lionel Messi (Argentina, mature, trimmed russet full beard, chestnut brushed quiff), Cristiano Ronaldo (Portugal, angular tanned cheekbones and clean shave, dark swept short quiff, brown eyes), Kylian Mbappé (France, young rounder cheeks, dense very short dark fade hair). Second row: Lamine Yamal (Spain, youthful face, dark fluffy coiled irregular fringe), Erling Haaland (Norway, long blond hair brushed straight back tied low, pale brows blue eyes, broad square chin), Vinícius Júnior (Brazil, high dark tight afro fade, prominent smile lines, slight goatee). Striking instantly recognizable real likeness, adult and age-appropriate young athlete features; handcrafted precision semi-realistic sports illustration with painterly planar modeling in warm chromatic values, fine eyebrows, expressive detailed eyes and hair strands, soft matte materials, natural skin. Neutral closed lips, eyes looking straight forward, symmetrical unrotated head exactly straight ahead orthographic, same eye height per row. Full crown and ears, tightly framed heads without neck or torso, each isolated against pure white solid. Compose equal box centres (1/6,3/6,5/6 by 1/4,3/4), similar head sizes occupying about 84% of cell height with white gutters. Neutral diffuse frontal illumination without directional shadows. No text, logos, graphics, frames, attire, or pedestals. An original cohesive character facial albedo design sheet for transfer onto sculpted game meshes.
