---
name: cup-economy-review
description: Compare a Continental Cup guaranteed payout with a risky shot, validate the catalogue and inspect model evidence.
---

Read the versioned Continental Cup catalogue through the Event Studio MCP resource. Use the current shot weights, cash and trophy value to explain the two endpoints of the safe-cash interval. Send candidate catalogues to the validator. For the cloud reviewer, read the alternative offers at `studio://review/brief`. Among valid alternatives choose the lowest payout. Call `verify_choice` and retain its structured verdict. Check the negative control. Review the evidence for each model, including rejected attempts and tokens consumed before acceptance. The current event changes only after an explicit catalogue edit, generation of Luau and a separate playtest.
